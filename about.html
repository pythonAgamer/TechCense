About TechCense

How we work, how we make money, and why you can trust us.

Our Mission

TechCense was founded to help people make smarter buying decisions. With hundreds of gadgets launching every year, it's hard to know what's actually worth your money. We cut through the marketing hype and give you honest, real-world reviews — tech that actually makes sense.

How We Test

Every gadget we review is personally used for a minimum of one week (often longer). We test in real-world conditions — not in a lab. That means we charge the battery, install the updates, and live with the device just like you would.

  • We buy most products ourselves.
  • We disclose when we receive review units.
  • We update reviews when prices or alternatives change.

How This Site Makes Money

This site uses affiliate links. If you click a link on our site and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. This is what keeps the site free and running.

Our recommendations are never influenced by commissions. We always call out flaws, even if it means losing a sale. Your trust is more important.
